## 📝 Overview  
World Environment Day was observed on **June 5, 2025**, at **Amrita Vishwa Vidyapeetham, Coimbatore campus** with a series of meaningful activities:  

- 🎨 **Digital poster-making competition** on *“End Plastic Pollution”* encouraged students to express creativity and sustainability awareness.  
- 🌱 **Tree-planting ceremony** reaffirmed the institution’s commitment to environmental conservation.  

The celebration was graced by **Campus Director Air Cmdr. Satish Menon**, along with senior faculty, staff, and students. Together, the Amrita community pledged to foster awareness and contribute to a greener, healthier future.  

---

## ⚙️ Planning & Execution  
- Conducted **digital poster-making competition** on the theme *“End Plastic Pollution”*.  
- Organized a **tree-planting ceremony** symbolizing sustainability.  
- Active participation by Campus Director, faculty, staff, and students.  
- **Winners recognized**; all participants appreciated.  
- Concluded with a **pledge for eco-friendly practices**.  

---

## 🌱 Impact & Outcomes  
The event successfully achieved its goals by fostering:  

- **Environmental Awareness** – through student engagement in sustainability-focused activities.  
- **Sustainable Practices** – reinforced by tree planting.  
- **Community Engagement** – uniting faculty, staff, and students.  
- **Inspiration for Action** – motivating eco-friendly lifestyle adoption.  

---

## 👥 Participation  
- **~50 students** and **~20 faculty/staff** actively participated.  
- Engagement included both **poster competition** and **tree-planting ceremony**.  
- Reflected **shared dedication to sustainability** and collective responsibility.  

---

## 🎯 Contribution to UN SDGs  
- **SDG 12 – Responsible Consumption & Production:** Promoting sustainable lifestyle choices.  
- **SDG 13 – Climate Action:** Tree-planting initiative reducing carbon footprint.  
- **SDG 15 – Life on Land:** Enhancing campus green cover and protecting ecosystems/biodiversity.
