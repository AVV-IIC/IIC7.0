## 📝 Overview  
On **August 14, 2025**, Amrita Vishwa Vidyapeetham celebrated World Entrepreneurs Day in alignment with **Gokulashtami** festivities by organizing a unique student Debate competition. The theme was to blend entrepreneurial lessons with cultural traditions and develop creative, strategic thinking skills.

- 180 students participated in prelims; 20 reached the finals.
- Debate Topic: "Was Lord Krishna the Earliest Known Strategic Entrepreneur?"
  - *For the Motion*: Krishna's leadership, networking, crisis management, and resource innovation.
  - *Against the Motion*: Krishna's actions guided by dharma and divine purpose rather than entrepreneurial principles.
- Debate topics were announced 45 minutes before commencement, balancing spontaneity with preparation.

Event was coordinated by Mr. Ramaguru Radhakrishnan (Assistant Professor, TIFAC-CORE Cyber Security & IIC Convener), with judging by Ms. Anjali B K (Learning Development) and Dr. Haripriya Ravikumar (Food Science Nutrition). Student coordinators managed flow and timekeeping.
