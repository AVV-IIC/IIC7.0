**Amrita Vishwa Vidyapeetham | Celebrations Activity Report**  

---

## 📌 Event Details  
- **Activity Category:** Celebrations  
- **Event Title:** International Women’s Day 2025  
- **Date:** March 8, 2025  
- **Theme/Occasion:** *International Women’s Day Celebrations*  
- **Organized by:** Office of Student Affairs (OSA) & WiCyS Amrita Student Chapter, in collaboration with IIC  

---

## 📝 Overview  
On **March 8, 2025**, Amrita Vishwa Vidyapeetham celebrated **International Women’s Day** with events that honored women’s achievements and promoted empowerment.  

- 🎖️ **Felicitation of Women Achievers** – Recognized women for contributions in research, societal service, and non-academic domains.  
- 🧠 **Cyber Quiz** – Engaged students in Women’s Day themes and Cyber Security through prelims and buzzer-based finals.  
- 🎤 **Panel Discussion: Breaking Barriers in Cybersecurity** – Featured distinguished women from academia and industry, sharing insights on careers, gender challenges, mentorship, skills, and ethics.  

Together, these initiatives celebrated women’s impact while advancing knowledge, innovation, and empowerment in line with the spirit of Women’s Day and IIC.  

---

## ⚙️ Planning & Execution  

1. **Felicitation of Women Achievers**  
   - Honorees identified across research, service, and non-academic pursuits.  
   - Opening by Dean of Student Affairs, citations, and stage recognition.  
   - Closing session with collective appreciation reinforcing Amrita’s commitment.  

2. **Cyber Quiz**  
   - 25 teams participated in a rapid-fire preliminary round (15 questions).  
   - Final round with top 6 teams included buzzer rounds.  
   - Quizmaster facilitated learning alongside competition.  

3. **Panel Discussion: Breaking Barriers in Cybersecurity**  
   - **Speakers:**  
     - Ms. Akshaya Rajendran – Email Security Lead, TCS  
     - Mrs. Jeyanthi Periasamy – Audit & Compliance Manager, IQVIA  
     - Ms. Purnima Gururanjan – Cyber Security Architect, DTICI & Amrita Alumnus  
     - Dr. Jevitha K P – Assistant Professor, TIFAC-CORE, Amrita  
   - Moderated by a student; covered mentorship, gender-based cybercrimes, skills, and ethics.  
   - Closing with vote of thanks by WiCyS President and felicitation of panelists.  

---

## 👥 Participation  

- **Felicitation Ceremony:** ~10 women achievers honored; ~50 faculty, staff, and students in attendance.  
- **Cyber Quiz:** 25 student teams (2 members each) participated, showcasing quick thinking and teamwork.  
- **Panel Discussion:** ~75 students (B.Tech CSE-CYS), faculty, and industry/academic experts engaged in discussions.  

Total **94 participants** including students, faculty, and external experts.  

---

## 🌱 Impact & Outcomes  

1. **Felicitation of Women Achievers**  
   - Celebrated women’s contributions across multiple domains.  
   - Inspired pride and motivation among the community.  

2. **Cyber Quiz**  
   - Enhanced knowledge of cybersecurity and women’s issues.  
   - Built quick decision-making, accuracy, and collaboration.  

3. **Panel Discussion**  
   - Created awareness on women’s role in cybersecurity.  
   - Fostered critical thinking and career inspiration.
