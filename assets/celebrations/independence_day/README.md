## 📝 Overview  
On **August 15, 2025**, Amrita Vishwa Vidyapeetham celebrated the **79th Independence Day** by honoring India’s spirit of freedom and unity. The occasion included memorable moments and unique initiatives:

- 🇮🇳 **Flag Hoisting** – Performed by Dr. P. Ajith Kumar, Registrar, followed by the National Anthem.
- 🎤 **Inspiring Addresses** – Dr. P. Ajith Kumar and Air Cmdr. Satish Menon (Campus Director) highlighted themes of freedom, responsibility, innovation, and the Viksit Bharat 2047 vision.
- 🎶 **Cultural Performances** – Patriotic songs, dances, and student speeches fostered national pride and unity.
- 🚀 **Program Inauguration** – Viksit Bharat Ambassador Youth Connect Program was formally launched with objectives outlined for future youth initiatives.
- 🧠 **India Quiz Competition** – Engaged nearly 500 students in patriotic, rapid-fire questions; promoted teamwork and knowledge.
- 🎉 **Community Engagement** – Over 75 faculty coordinated the event to ensure smooth execution.

---

## ⚙️ Planning & Execution  

1. **Flag Hoisting & National Anthem**  
   - Led by Registrar Dr. P. Ajith Kumar, followed by community singing.
2. **Ceremonial Welcome & Messages**  
   - Welcome by Ms. Aswathi K. B. (Head, Student Welfare). Independence Day and Viksit Bharat objectives articulated by leadership.
3. **Cultural Programs**  
   - Dance, music, and speech segments offered students a platform for patriotic expression.
4. **Inauguration & Vision Session**  
   - Campus Director and organising committee introduced Viksit Bharat Youth Connect and outlined the roadmap towards 2047.
5. **India Quiz Competition**  
   - Open to student groups; encouraged participation, accuracy, and collaboration.
